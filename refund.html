14

If you are not satisfied for any reason, you may request a full refund within 14 days of your initial purchase or renewal date — no questions asked.

How to Request a Refund

Email support@sharedvisionai.org with the subject line "Refund Request" and your registered email address. We will process your request within 5 business days.

Refunds are processed by Paddle as our Merchant of Record and may take 5–10 business days to appear on your statement, depending on your payment method and bank.

Conditions

  • Limit of one refund per customer
  • Annual subscriptions: eligible for refund within 14 days of the purchase or renewal date only

After the Refund Window

After 14 days, no refunds are issued except where required by applicable law (including EU consumer protection rights where applicable).
