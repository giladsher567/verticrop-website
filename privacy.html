1. Who We Are

VertiCrop is operated by Gilad Shar, a sole proprietor based in Israel.

2. What Data We Collect

  • Email address (required for account creation)
  • Password (stored as a one-way hash — we never see your actual password)
  • IP address (logged temporarily for security purposes)
  • App usage events: account creation, logins, license validations — no video content

We do not collect, store, or process any video files you create with VertiCrop.

3. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)

  • Performance of contract: email and password are necessary to provide the service
  • Legitimate interests: IP logging for security and fraud prevention

4. How We Use Your Data

  • To authenticate you and provide access to VertiCrop
  • To manage your subscription
  • To contact you about your account (no marketing without your consent)

5. Third Parties

  • Paddle.com (Merchant of Record): processes your payment. When you purchase a subscription, Paddle collects your payment details directly. We share your email with Paddle solely to link your payment to your account. See Paddle's privacy policy.
  • Contabo GmbH (Hosting): our license server is hosted on Contabo's infrastructure in Germany.

We do not sell your data to any third party.

6. Data Retention

  • Account data: retained while your account is active, plus 30 days after deletion request
  • Activity logs (IP, login events): 90 days
  • Deleted accounts: email and password hash are permanently deleted upon request

7. Your Rights

Under GDPR and applicable law, you have the right to:

  • Access — request a copy of your personal data
  • Rectification — correct inaccurate data
  • Erasure — request deletion of your account and data
  • Portability — receive your data in a machine-readable format
  • Restriction — limit how we process your data
  • Objection — object to processing based on legitimate interests

To exercise any right, contact support@sharedvisionai.org. We will respond within 30 days.

8. Security

Passwords are hashed using bcrypt. All data is transmitted over HTTPS. We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data.

9. Children

VertiCrop is not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. We will notify registered users by email of any material changes.
